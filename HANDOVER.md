## The design the user has fixed (Component 1)
- Each Karandaaz phase runs in two sub-phases: **Readiness** (100% in person, cohort of 30, one trainer, registration on the
  SDPI DFLT App) → 4 weeks digital reinforcement → **Capability** one month later (hands-on: transaction simulation on trainer
  tablets replicating the district's assigned bank wallet, trainer-vs-beneficiary role play with the PKR 1,000 agent fee,
  5 groups, payment-journey puzzle worksheet, right/wrong pictorial worksheet) → 4 more weeks reinforcement.
  Capability sub-phase is **70:30 physical:digital per province as a whole** (districts vary); the 30% are women the trainer
  judges already capable, confirmed by a matrix in the App (own phone, education, prior transactions, task check).
- Segmentation layers: geography (urban/rural tehsil), device (smartphone vs USSD; own vs household access), capability.
- Digital package: smartphone → 3 videos + 4 WhatsApp messages per 4-week window, **no IVR**; USSD → 4 IVR + 3 SMS, **no video**;
  robocalls + human follow-up calls to all. Trainer helps open WhatsApp where needed.
- Baseline: qualitative FGDs only, no survey, no endline of its own; 8 zones (Punjab N/C/S, KP N/S, Sindh Upper/Lower, Quetta),
  1 district per zone, 1 urban + 1 rural tehsil each (16 tehsils); presented at the inception meeting in Month 1;
  questionnaires of Pilot, DFLT I, DFLT II to be attached as Annex A (user will provide).
- Capability Ladder (10 steps) answers Karandaaz's "where do beneficiaries stand on 1–10".
- Languages: Urdu, Sindhi, Pashto, Brahvi, Marwari. 20-page pictorial handbook condensed from SDPI's 72-page Phase II handbook
  (72 pages is from the user's brief, not a document). 12 DFLT I videos: https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/14YuNgn4AHUPPUru1nSN6D8wzhhsuMiLw
- Gender: characters Naila & Shumaila, Akbari & Asghari carry all content. "Well received by beneficiaries" is the user's claim.
- Peer educator: one woman per community (trainer's household/community or a beneficiary's literate daughter) plus a monthly
  community session at a payment point in the BISP payment window.

## Writing style the user requires (they rejected several drafts over this)
- Model: `Karandaaz/DFLT Phase 2 Proposal.docx`. Connected explanatory paragraphs that state who does what, in what numbers,
  through which mechanism, and why. Programme-descriptive voice ("The program will…", "BISP will be requested to…").
- Not a report of what is happening, not a tutorial, not a sales pitch: no "SDPI brings…", "proven", "widely popular",
  no slogans ("tasks rather than concepts"), no promises beyond evidence ("every trained woman will…").
- Few sub-headings: keep the RFP's (a)–(h) headings only; bold lead labels at most for short method paragraphs.
- Concise and precise; component ≤ ~10 pages; verified numbers only, with footnotes; tables and figures where they carry the argument.
- Do not rewrite the whole document for a local complaint; fix the paragraph named.
