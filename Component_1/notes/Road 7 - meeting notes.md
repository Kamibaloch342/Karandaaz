## Decisions

1. **Digital reinforcement window is four weeks, not six** (≈03:30–04:30, 11:00, 17:00).
   "Six weeks is too much" — after the readiness session, four weeks of digital content, then
   the capability session. Trainer workload stays the same.
2. **Human follow-up calls from a call centre** (≈00:30–03:00, 15:00).
   - Example: in Punjab 150 trainers × 30 women = 4,500 women trained per day.
   - A cohort trained on 1 January starts receiving digital content on 2 January; its first
     human call goes on 5 January.
   - One caller makes about 200 calls a day, so 4,500 ÷ 200 = 22.5 → 22–23 callers for Punjab.
   - Summed across provinces the team arrived at roughly 57 callers for Phase 1
     (≈ 348 trainers × 30 ÷ 200 = 52, plus margin) *(figure partly unclear)*.
   - Calls run cohort by cohort through the month; the first month is the heaviest.
3. **Trainer rhythm** (≈08:00, 17:00, 22:00): 16 working days (16 sessions) a month per
   trainer; 16 × 30 = 480 women a month. Phase 1 uses about 348 trainers.
4. **Mop-up window before capability** (≈05:00–07:00, 16:30, 21:30): 1–15 February is used to
   clear the backlog (women who could not be reached or missed a session); women who miss
   their capability session have until about mid-March, so the phase closes within about
   three months.
5. **Rolling cohorts** (≈17:00–19:00): in March trainers start readiness for new cohorts,
   whose capability sessions run mid-April to mid-May.
6. **Digital Capability Track (30%)** (≈08:30, 14:00): women on the digital track are not
   called to the physical capability session; the monitoring team tracks them separately and
   they receive capability content digitally.
7. **Assisted transactions at payment points** (≈18:00, 23:00–26:30):
   - During the BISP payment window, trainers stand at payment points (retail agents / BISP
     sites) for about four hours and help any woman who asks, not only their own trainees.
   - Women are told during the capability session where in their tehsil the trainer will be.
   - Each assisted transaction is logged in the DFLT App (name and CNIC, with a photo/record
     where possible).
   - SDPI triangulates these records against its trained database and sends the names and
     CNICs to BISP to verify that the transactions went through without a grievance.
8. **Field structure** (≈30:30–31:30): at most four trainers per tehsil; the IC visits for
   about 16 days a month *(partly unclear)*.
9. **Audio audits** (≈27:30): 100% recording of 348 trainers × 16 sessions is a limit; audits
   will be sampled.
